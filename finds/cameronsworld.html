Cameron's World

Added to The Forgotten Web: 2026-10-21. Historical publication details follow.

August 2015 · GeoCities / web collage · original online
WhatInteractive collage assembled from archived GeoCities pages.
Why nowThe personal web was a place to make a world, not just fill out a profile.
Statusoriginal online · individual archive links may vary
Found inpre-platform culture

Cameron Askin excavated text and images from GeoCities archives and assembled them into a towering, clickable landscape: glittering galaxies, cats, dragons, homemade shrines, and introductions by people who had something wonderfully specific to put online. Published in August 2015, it is a new artwork made from older web fragments, not an untouched 1990s homepage. Follow its links to the archived neighbourhoods behind the collage.

WHY IT STILL MATTERSWhere a platform gives everyone the same profile box, these fragments preserve the impulse to build a room of your own. This is not an essay about the lost human web; it lets you wander through its unruly visual language.
“A love letter to the Internet of old” — the project's own introduction.
