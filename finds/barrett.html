Cameron Barrett, the person behind CamWorld, described weblogs as a living pattern: frequent updates, links out, commentary, personality, and a visible trail through the web.

WHY IT STILL MATTERSBefore platforms converted discovery into ranking, weblogs were people manually wiring the web together. This is a field sketch of that mechanism.
