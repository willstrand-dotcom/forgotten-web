Warner Bros.’ 1996 Space Jam site is still sitting there in the open: black starfields, image-map navigation, loud table layouts, tiny GIF-era spectacle, and source comments that sound like people building a movie lobby out of HTML by hand.

WHY IT STILL MATTERSMost promotional sites are disposable campaign mulch. This one survived as a working fossil from the moment corporations were discovering the web without yet flattening it into a CMS-approved landing page.
Best opened when the modern web feels too smooth. This is not elegant; it is alive, specific, and wearing fluorescent sneakers.
