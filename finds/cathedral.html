Eric S. Raymond watched Linux and fetchmail turn software development inside out: frequent releases, distributed debugging, visible source, and users treated as co-developers instead of spectators.

WHY IT STILL MATTERSOpen source is now everywhere, but often as invisible infrastructure or corporate slogan. This essay preserves the earlier cultural argument: messy public collaboration can outbuild carefully managed cathedrals when the feedback loops are honest.
Best opened when “community” has started meaning a Discord channel attached to a product roadmap.
